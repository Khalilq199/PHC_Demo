# PHC patient dashboard

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, plain CSS. Chosen under the Step 1 instruction to select a simple frontend for an empty repository.

## Users and purpose

Patients reviewing complex longitudinal care, with a clinician lens over the same patient story. Help the patient understand current priorities, changes, next actions, and supporting evidence.

## Capabilities and constraints

Step 2 extends Today with Blood Pressure detail, an evidence drawer with source records, Health over time with topic filters, and a clinician lens. No backend, authentication, medical recommendations, diagnosis, or live patient information. Vitamin D and thyroid priority links lead to their filtered histories; no separate detail pages are required.

## Evidence on hand

The supplied steps/00_CONTEXT.md, steps/01_BUILD_CORE_APP.md, and steps/02_BUILD_INTERACTIONS.md define all synthetic patient facts and copy. Maya Patel is fictional. Recorded values and existing care-plan actions do not imply a new clinical assessment. The blood pressure PHC summary was reviewed September 20, 2026; the priority still needs follow-up. Unspecified review/ownership details remain unspecified. Only the seven supplied home readings have individual values; the historical baseline remains a summary of 18 observations.

## Brand commitments

Precision Health Centre (PHC). Calm, restrained clinical interface; readable typography, forest green accent, neutral surfaces, minimal decoration. The browser implementation is visual authority; no Figma reproduction.

## Product principles

Priority first. Trend and treatment together. Next action always visible. Blood pressure evidence one interaction away from its detail view. Text accompanies every status color.
