# Step 2 interaction extension

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing Today surface and visual system. The coded browser implementation remains visual authority.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep priority, change, action, and supporting evidence connected across the same patient story. Product depth comes from traceable data and treatment chronology.

OWN-WORLD: Retain Public Sans, forest green, neutral backgrounds, thin borders, and the editorial priority sheet. New surfaces inherit the existing tokens. No redesign, image comps, decorative widgets, gradients, or shadows.

STORY: Enter Blood Pressure from Today, inspect the baseline-relative chart and existing plan, then open the evidence and its recorded sources. Switch to Clinician for counts, relevant context, and unresolved workflow questions. Use Health over time to connect results with care events; the Vitamin D filter makes the intervention sequence explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the compact header and greeting. Enrich Today with context metadata and small data-driven sequences. Blood Pressure pairs a wide chart with a narrower next-action column, followed by clinician context when selected. The evidence drawer overlays the right side and scrolls independently. Timeline filters precede chronological records.

FORM: Extension of the approved Step 1 editorial composition. No concept seed or replacement world. Reuse the existing data objects and light visual system.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Data and state decisions

- Hash navigation encodes page, lens, and topic filter so refresh and browser history work.
- The native modal dialog blocks background interaction, cycles keyboard focus, supports Escape/backdrop/close, and returns focus to its opener. Source records are nested states in the same drawer.
- Summary review status is separate from priority follow-up status.
- The baseline chart is an aggregate over Apr–Jun; dashed connections are explicitly labeled as summaries, not fabricated monthly measurements.
- The Vitamin D sequence shows timing after supplementation without claiming causality.
